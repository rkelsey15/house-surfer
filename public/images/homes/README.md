# Sample cover photos for real homes

Put your own house photos (10 to 15) in this folder, then list them in `covers.json`.
Each home that has a sample cover shows that photo instead of the drawing, until a
visitor snaps their own photo of it. Steps and rules are in `docs/STATUS.md`
(section "Adding your own house photos").

- Only photos you took yourself, from a public sidewalk. No listing photos, no stock photos.
- No people, no license plates, no house numbers close up.
- JPEG, about 1200 pixels wide, under about 250 KB each.
- Remove location data first (see the steps in STATUS.md).
