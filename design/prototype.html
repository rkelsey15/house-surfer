House Surfer

Rate houses on a walk with friends or from the couch. Tap through the phone yourself, or press Play for a guided 40-second demo.

9:415G ▮▮▮
Try itPick a handle, rate a few homes, open one, post a comment, or tap the camera to rate a house you're walking past.
  1. Pick an anonymous handle
  2. Surf the feed and rate 1 to 5 stars
  3. Open a home, read and post comments
  4. Snap a house you're walking past
  5. Find homes on the map
  6. See your karma and badges

Tip: try posting a comment that mentions the people who live there to see the moderation screen.

House Surfer is an original concept prototype for a portfolio case study. All homes, ratings, handles and comments are made up. Homes are illustrations until a user adds a photo.
