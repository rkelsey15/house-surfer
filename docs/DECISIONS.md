# Decisions

Append-only. One line each: date, decision, why. Dates before Nov 15, 2026 were not recorded.

- Nov 15, 2026: Townhouses stay in `homes.json` but are hidden by the `showTownhouses` flag. Why: houses-only launch with a one-line way to turn them on.
- Nov 15, 2026: Pins are blurred 25 to 50 m and the exact point is never saved. Why: exact pins would undo the block-level address rule.
- Nov 15, 2026: Style is an era tag from year built. Why: county data has no architectural style.
- Nov 15, 2026: Houses are drawn in code (`HouseArt`), no listing photos. Why: rule against listing data and photos.
- Nov 15, 2026: "Takes" renamed to "comments" everywhere. Why: clearer for new users.
- Nov 15, 2026: Comments use `parent_id` threads, an edit-history table and a likes table; counts are computed, not stored. Why: stable ids, and the same shape V2's database would use.
- Nov 15, 2026: Soft delete with `deleted_at`. Why: keeps replies, edit history and likes.
- Nov 15, 2026: Replies are one level deep in the UI. Why: deep threads get cramped on phones.
- Nov 15, 2026: Handles are generated and locked after sign-up. Why: makes real names and slurs impossible instead of filtering them.
- Nov 15, 2026: Pin colors are hollow gray, blue, green, gold (4.5+ with 3+ ratings), flame, purple crown. Why: gold is earned, and red or orange read as "bad".
- Nov 15, 2026: Router is `HashRouter`. Why: GitHub Pages works without server rules.
- Nov 15, 2026: Everything in the browser uses `hs.` keys through `src/lib/storage.js`. Why: one pattern, safe if storage is blocked.
- Nov 15, 2026: Deploy before Snap a house. Why: phone camera and location only work on a real https site.
- Nov 15, 2026: V2 stays a paper design, no paid database. Why: the portfolio goal is showing the thinking at $0.
- Nov 15, 2026: Git commits use the GitHub noreply address; history was rewritten and force-pushed. Why: public repo, protect the personal email.
- Nov 22, 2026: House Surfer is a portfolio demo, not a live app. Anyone with the link can play in about 60 seconds, with no accounts, database or paid services; everything stays in the visitor's browser. Why: lowest friction for recruiters and friends, $0 to run, and the product thinking still shows in the docs, metrics and moderation.
- Nov 22, 2026: Adopted the PM lifecycle workflow: `docs/STATUS.md` and `docs/DECISIONS.md`, short `CLAUDE.md`. Why: keeps new sessions cheap and the project documented.
- Nov 22, 2026: The demo note is a top strip on phones and a floating pill at bottom-left on laptops. Why: on laptops the tabs sit in the top bar and covered a top strip.
- Nov 22, 2026: "Reset demo" asks first, clears only keys starting with `hs.`, then reloads to the handle picker. Why: one safe wipe that resets every screen's state, and nothing outside the app is touched.
- Nov 22, 2026: "Try this" checkmarks only turn on, never off, and live in `hs.guide`. Why: deleting a comment shouldn't undo progress.
- Nov 22, 2026: Moderation is rules only for the demo, with the word lists in their own file (`src/lib/moderation-words.js`). Why: easy to review and edit without touching logic; an AI check is a V2 item.
- Nov 22, 2026: Moderation matches whole words, undoes spacing, number swaps and repeated letters, and treats hyphenated words as one word. "family room", "family home" and "neighborhood" pass. Why: keep real house comments working. Trade-off: "owner-occupied" slips through and a few harmless phrases get blocked.
- Nov 22, 2026: The same check runs on new comments, replies and edits. Why: an edit would otherwise be a way around the rule.
- Nov 22, 2026: Dropped "couple", "creeps" and "freak" from the word lists. Why: "a couple of windows" and "ivy creeps up the wall" are normal house comments.
- Nov 22, 2026: A blocked comment logs `comment_blocked` with only a reason (`person` or `insult`), never the text. Why: the guardrail metric needs counts, not content. The small event logger was built early for this; the rest of the events and `/stats` come in section 7.
- Nov 22, 2026: Moderation tests run with Node's built-in runner (`npm test`), no new packages. Why: free, and the 44 examples double as documentation.
- Nov 22, 2026: Accessibility target is WCAG 2.2 AA, with the list view equal to the map, pin shapes not just colors, screen-reader labels and 44 px tap targets. The basics get built in the UI polish pass; "Hear this house" is a future feature. Why: an accessible demo reaches more visitors and is a stronger portfolio piece. Documented in the PRD, not claimed as met until audited.
- Nov 22, 2026: Two contrast bugs (reset button, blocked message) were fixed with a new `--danger` color. Why: both were under the 4.5:1 text standard in light mode.
- Nov 22, 2026: Snap a house shows "Take a photo" and "Choose from library" on touch devices, and "Upload a photo" on laptops; "Use a sample photo" is on both. Why: a recruiter on a laptop has no house photo handy, and a phone visitor may not either.
- Nov 22, 2026: The sample photo is a drawing made in code and labeled "Sample drawing, not a real home". Why: no listing photos and no outside image licenses (rule 4); it still goes through the real shrink and save steps.
- Nov 22, 2026: Photos are redrawn on a canvas at up to 1200 px and saved as new JPEGs. Why: a canvas copy keeps only pixels, so GPS and other hidden camera data are dropped; tested with a file carrying fake GPS data.
- Nov 22, 2026: Location is asked for only when the visitor taps "Use my location", is used once to sort the 5 nearest homes, and is never saved or logged. Why: privacy rule, and a button tap makes the browser prompt less surprising.
- Nov 22, 2026: If the visitor is more than 1.5 km from any home (for example a recruiter in another city), "near me" says the demo only covers Queen Anne and switches to address search. Why: showing homes from across the country as "nearest" would look broken.
- Nov 22, 2026: Saving a photo needs a checked box "This photo has no people or license plates", beside a visible "No people or license plates" notice. Why: the PRD says people and plates must not be photographed; with no photo screening in the demo, the confirm is the control.
- Nov 22, 2026: Photos are saved in IndexedDB (database `house-surfer`) and "Reset demo" deletes the whole database. Why: photos are too big for localStorage; deleting the database is the simplest way to guarantee nothing is left.
- Nov 22, 2026: The first photo saved for a home is its cover everywhere (feed, home page, list); further photos show in a strip on the home page. Why: matches the PRD ("first approved photo becomes the cover").
- Nov 22, 2026: The Top screen reads the same stats as the map pins (`useHomeStats`), and the numbers behind gold, hot and home of the week now live in one file (`src/lib/rules.js`). Why: the Top screen and the map can never disagree, and the rules are easy to find and change.
- Nov 22, 2026: Top rated uses the gold rule on its own (4.5+ with 3+ ratings), not "gold or crown". Why: the crown home can have a lower average than 4.5, and a "4.5 or more" list shouldn't contain it.
- Nov 22, 2026: "Hot this week" is ranked by recent activity (ratings and comments in 7 days), then average. Why: the flame has a threshold but no order; "most active first" matches the Top screen's promise.
- Nov 22, 2026: Top lists show 10 rows with "Show more" instead of all rows. Why: thousands of homes can be rated or hot in the sample data and one long page is slow and unreadable on a phone.
- Nov 22, 2026: The iPhone test of Snap a house is pending, and work continues on the next sections. Why: Ryan can't test on a phone yet; everything else about Snap was verified in headless Edge.
- Nov 22, 2026: "Hot this week" shows only the top 5 (no "Show more"). Why: Ryan wanted it to feel special; in the sample data 10+ homes are hot at any time.
- Nov 22, 2026: Recent activity on Profile is built from the saved data (ratings, my comments, my likes, photos on this device), not from the event log. Why: the event log doesn't record ratings or likes yet (that is section 7), and building from the data always matches what the app holds. Likes show the home they were on, not the comment text.
- Nov 22, 2026: Profile lists show only live comments; deleted ones are hidden. Why: deleted comments no longer earn karma or show on home pages, so they shouldn't show on Profile either.
- Nov 22, 2026: Five placeholder badges (First Steps, Block Walker, Good Neighbor Voice, Crowd Favorite, Curb Photographer) are shown locked and marked "Coming soon". Why: the criteria need only locked placeholders; names are in `src/lib/profileLists.js` and are easy to change (open question on the first badge set remains).
- Nov 22, 2026: Events are logged where the action happens (the ratings, comments and theme code), not from screens. Why: one place per action, so no screen can forget it. Likes log only when a like is added, not removed.
- Nov 22, 2026: `home_viewed` source is passed through the link ("feed", "map", "search", "top", "snap"); a direct link or refresh logs "other". Map list and map card count as "search" while the search box has text, else "map". Why: matches the PRD's source field without a new tracking layer.
- Nov 22, 2026: On `/stats`, a "week" is the last 7 days counted back from now (this week, last week, and so on), not Monday to Sunday. Why: simpler, no calendar edge cases, and the same in every time zone. Ratings counted include changed ratings (`rating_submitted` with `is_edit`).
- Nov 22, 2026: `/stats` reads the event log once when it opens (no live refresh). Why: it is a report, and reopening it is enough.
- Nov 22, 2026: New order of work: UI polish (Part A, then Part B), then the case study, then PRD Appendix G last. Why: the accessibility results and screenshots feed the case study, and Appendix G is docs only.
- Nov 23, 2026: Pins are drawn by a small custom Leaflet marker (`ShapeMarker` in MapScreen) on the same canvas, not as 5,000 HTML markers. Why: shapes were needed without slowing the map. It relies on two private Leaflet 1.9 methods (`_updatePath`, `_fillStroke`), so a Leaflet upgrade needs a map check.
- Nov 23, 2026: Shapes: hollow circle, circle, square, star, diamond (plus flame and crown badges). The shape table lives in `src/lib/pins.js` and the legend is built from it. Why: one source for map and legend, and it is testable.
- Nov 23, 2026: Map pins are not keyboard-reachable; the List view is the equal alternative, and the map is labeled that way. Pins get a wider click area (10 px) but stay below 44 px. Why: a canvas of 5,000 points can't be a Tab stop each; WCAG 2.5.8 allows this when the same action exists another way.
- Nov 23, 2026: Added color tokens `--control-line` (3:1 borders for inputs and buttons) and `--gold-text`; darkened `--muted` and `--gold` in light mode; the karma pill uses `--danger`. Why: a contrast check found text and star colors under 4.5:1 and input borders under 3:1. `tests/contrast.test.js` now guards both themes.
- Nov 23, 2026: Empty stars are outlines and filled stars are solid. Why: state is not shown by color or fade alone (WCAG 1.4.1).
- Nov 23, 2026: Stars are plain buttons in a group with "pressed" state, not radio buttons. Why: radios imply arrow keys that select, which would log a rating on every arrow press.
- Nov 23, 2026: The blocked-comment box takes focus and no longer uses `role="alert"`. Why: moving focus makes the screen reader read it once; both together would read it twice.
- Nov 23, 2026: The laptop demo note shrinks to an "i" button after 5 seconds, but not while the pointer or keyboard focus is on it, and tapping "i" moves focus into the note. Why: it stops covering the list, and nobody loses it mid-read (WCAG 2.2.1 spirit). On phones it stays a strip.
- Nov 23, 2026: The tab bar comes before the page in the code and a "Skip to content" button was added. Why: on laptops the tabs are at the top, so Tab order now matches what you see.
- Nov 23, 2026: Three button styles: primary (filled), secondary (outlined, the default `.btn`) and quiet (no box, brand-colored text). Icon-only buttons are one 44 px circle (`.icon-btn`). Why: one clear rule for what a button looks like; the old `.link-btn` is gone.
- Nov 23, 2026: Corner radius scale as tokens: 8 (small things), 12 (buttons, inputs, chips, tabs), 16 (cards and panels); circles and the two status pills stay round. Filter chips changed from pills to 12 px. The logo mark keeps its 8 px. Why: "same corner radius everywhere"; `tests/style.test.js` fails if a stray radius appears.
- Nov 23, 2026: One icon set with a 2 px round line, and text characters (x, arrows, check, lock) replaced by icons. The legend and map badges (flame, crown) are not part of it: they are filled map symbols. Why: matching stroke weight; the glyphs rendered differently on each device.
- Nov 23, 2026: Phone map filters are one row that scrolls sideways (not a Filters button), and the Map/List switch moved up beside the search box. Why: filters stay one tap away and the toolbar drops from about a quarter to about an eighth of the screen. A cut-off chip at the screen edge is the hint that the row scrolls.
- Nov 23, 2026: Type scale 24/18/16/14/12.5 and spacing scale 4/8/12/16/24 live as CSS tokens (`--fs-*`, `--s1` to `--s5`). The home page address is the page's h1; the feed card address is an h2 under a hidden "Surf" h1. Why: a clear size ladder and a sensible heading outline for screen readers.
- Nov 23, 2026: "200% zoom" is checked as a 640 px wide window (a 1280 px window at 200%) and 320 px wide (400%), not by driving the browser's zoom setting. Why: the page layout responds to width, so these are equivalent for what the page sees; a hands-on browser zoom check is still on the list.
- Nov 23, 2026: The demo strip's height is published as a CSS variable (`--banner-h`) so the fixed Map screen starts below it. Why: fixes the search box hiding under the app bar on phones.
